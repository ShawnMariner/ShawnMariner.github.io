PROJECT OVERVIEW

Understanding rover-terrain interaction.

The project focuses on researching bevameter methods used to characterize the interaction between rover systems and granular terrain.

The instrument is being designed to measure pressure-sinkage and shear behavior in granular terrain relevant to rover mobility, while integrating into an existing rover wheel test platform.

02

OBJECTIVE

Expand the capabilities of an existing test platform.

The goal is to integrate a dedicated bevameter capability into a rover wheel test rig, allowing the research platform to support additional terramechanics experiments beyond wheel testing.

The design process involves investigating established bevameter testing methods and translating those requirements into a mechanical system that can be incorporated into the existing rig.
