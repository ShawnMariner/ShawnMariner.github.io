PROJECT OVERVIEW

Building a functional mobile system.

The Over Terrain Vehicle was a team engineering project focused on designing, building, and testing a small vehicle for a defined mobility challenge.

My primary responsibility was within the propulsion subsystem, where I focused on motor selection and supported the electrical and mechanical integration required to make the vehicle move.
